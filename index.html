    1. Click on the Expenses tab.
    2. Click Create Report (name the report, e.g. "Sampark Travel").
    3. Click on the Expense tab and upload the bill.
    4. Select the appropriate category (e.g. Travel and Accommodation), then select expense type, date, and enter other required details, and submit.
    5. Click the 3 dots on the left, add the expense to the report created, and click Submit.

    Password Reset Link

    If you forgot your password

    1. Once the link is open, click the "Forgot your password?" option.
    2. Enter your username and click Continue.
    3. Choose Authenticator or Security Question, then click Continue.
    4. For Authenticator: enter the code generated on your mobile. For Security Question: answer the question set by you.
    5. Click Submit.

    Employee Benefits

    Benefits info and Employee Club

    Tenure-Wise Benefits

    1–2 Years

    • Salary Advance Loan: For medical emergencies; up to ₹1 lakh or 2 months' fixed gross salary
    • Education Reimbursement: Up to ₹2 lakh for eligible courses; includes up to 10% for study materials
    • Internal Job Promotion (IJP): Eligible after 9 months of continuous service
    • Samvardhan Program: Identified for succession planning and leadership pipeline

    2–4 Years

    • Increased Education Support: Up to ₹3 lakh reimbursement depending on course duration
    • Salary Advance Loan: 3-month fixed gross salary or ₹1 lakh, whichever is lesser

    4+ Years

    • Education Reimbursement: Up to ₹4 lakh plus 10% for study materials

    Level-Wise Benefits

    Below Manager

    • Mobile Reimbursement: Case-based
    • Car Lease (Team Leader & above): After 1 year; lease up to ₹3.5 lakh or 45% of fixed CTC

    Manager / Sr. Manager

    • Mobile Handset: Reimbursed up to ₹20,000
    • Mobile Bill: Up to ₹1,000/month
    • Car Lease: After 6 months; lease up to 60% of fixed CTC

    AVP to EVP

    • Mobile Handset: Reimbursed up to ₹40,000
    • Mobile Bill: Up to ₹1,000/month
    • Car Lease: Same as above, up to 60% of fixed CTC

    AD & Above

    • Mobile & Bills: As per actuals
    • Car Lease: Same as above, up to 60% of fixed CTC
