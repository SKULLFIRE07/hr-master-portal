## Stack
Static HTML, CSS, JavaScript and JSON, as explicitly specified by the supplied PRD. GitHub Pages hosting; Python generates static PNG and SVG QR files.

Employees seeking payroll, IT, medical, sales and HR support, primarily on phones reached from a QR code. HR maintains links and escalation contacts.
## Product Purpose
One public link, no portal sign-in, five accordion sections with two subtabs each. Common information accessible in at most three taps.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Exact information architecture, contacts, system font, blue light/dark tokens, single column maximum 820px, and RAP arithmetic are pinned by PRD version 1.0 dated 15 October 2026. No browser storage or personal data on portal. Ticket destinations, incentive calculator and several contacts remain unprovided: never fabricate them or claim ticket delivery. External systems may require login. Publication on GitHub Pages explicitly requested by user.
## Evidence on Hand
User supplied HR-Master-Portal-PRD.md. Prototype zip and original calculator HTML were not supplied; recreate from documented behavior.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard accessible accordions/tabs, minimum 40px targets, AA contrast, visible focus, reduced motion, 360px mobile layout and device light/dark mode.
